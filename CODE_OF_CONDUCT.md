# Code of Conduct

Hearthly adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.
The full text is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>; this is a summary.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation — and to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: empathy and kindness, respect for differing
opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility and apologizing for
mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes: sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention; trolling,
insulting or derogatory comments and personal or political attacks; public or private harassment; publishing
others' private information without explicit permission; and other conduct which could reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer via a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/amanjhacodes55/hearthly/security/advisories/new) (mark it as a
conduct report) or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed
promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement ladder: **1. Correction**, **2. Warning**,
**3. Temporary ban**, **4. Permanent ban**, depending on the severity and pattern of the behavior.
